# Luma Lines — Privacy Policy

> **Plain-English summary**
>
> - Luma Lines collects no personal data.
> - Your game progress and settings stay on your device only.
> - We do not share, sell, or send your data to anyone.
> - There are no ads, no analytics, and no accounts.
> - Questions? Email shianux@gmail.com.

## About this policy

This policy explains what the Luma Lines app does with data. It applies to the Luma Lines app on iOS and on Android. Luma Lines is a calm block puzzle game. The app works without an internet connection.

## Who runs Luma Lines

Si Han Xu operates Luma Lines. The operator is in Singapore. For any privacy question, contact the operator at shianux@gmail.com.

## What "personal data" means

Personal data is information about you. Examples are your name, your email address, and your location. This policy tells you what personal data the app collects. The short answer is none.

## What we collect

We do not collect personal data. The app has no accounts and no sign-in. The app has no analytics tools and no advertising tools. The app has no crash reporting tools. The app does not send your data over the internet. It makes no network requests at all.

## What stays on your device

The app saves your game progress on your device. The app also saves your settings on your device. This data uses local storage on the device. This data never leaves the device.

## How long we keep your data

The app keeps your game data until you erase it. You erase this data when you delete the app. We hold no server copies, because the app has no servers.

## Sharing with others

We do not sell your data. We do not rent your data. We do not share your data with third parties. No third-party tools work inside the app. We cannot see your game data, because it never reaches us.

## Children

Luma Lines is suitable for children. Apple rates the app 4+. We will answer Google's content-rating questionnaire. We expect the rating "Everyone". We do not collect data from children, because we do not collect data from anyone. If a child uses the app, no personal data leaves the device.

## Permissions

The app saves your game data in its own private storage on the device. The app does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, or location. Private app storage needs no permission.

## Singapore law

The operator of Luma Lines is in Singapore. The main data protection law in Singapore is the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). We do not collect personal data through the app. In our view, this keeps our duties under the PDPA small. This statement is our own reading of the law. It is not legal advice.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page. We will also change the effective date below. You can check this page at any time for the current version.
